# Publications

This directory contains public-facing publications related to the Babak AI Engineering Standard (BAES) and its broader engineering context.

## Publications

### When Practical Engineering Advice Does Not Become a Foundation
**Subtitle:** A Bounded Candidate Review Within the Babak AI Engineering Standard (BAES)

An exploratory conceptual review of practical engineering recommendations, examining whether any of the reviewed candidates provides sufficient grounds for introducing an additional independent Foundation Model within BAES.

The paper distinguishes practical usefulness from foundational necessity, sets out qualitative candidate-assessment criteria, considers alternative architectural placements, and states the limits of its conclusions.

**Main finding:** Within the bounded candidate set examined, no candidate established sufficient grounds for an additional independent Foundation Model under the criteria applied. This finding does not establish that the existing BAES architecture is complete.

[Read the publication](When_Practical_Engineering_Advice_Does_Not_Become_a_Foundation.md)

## Scope and interpretation

The publications in this directory are intended for public discussion and should be interpreted within their stated scope and limitations. An exploratory analysis of a bounded set of candidates is not, by itself, a validation of the complete BAES architecture or a change to its normative baseline.

Unless a publication explicitly states otherwise, its findings should not be read as formal compliance determinations, empirical validation, or a claim that the architecture is complete.
